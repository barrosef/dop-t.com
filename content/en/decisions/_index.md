An architecture decision record fixes *what* was decided and describes the
architecture that follows — nothing else. These pages carry the reasoning
around each one: the question as it arrived, the options that were on the
table, the one taken and why, what the choice costs, and what has changed
since. Each is written from the decision as it stands today, and links to
the record it explains.
